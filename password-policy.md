# Example password and access policy

**Scope:** all employees and contractors. *(Template with fictional content.)*

1. **Length:** at least 14 characters; passphrases encouraged. No forced periodic change without suspicion of compromise (ANSSI / NIST guidance).
2. **Uniqueness:** one password per service; use of an approved password manager.
3. **MFA:** mandatory for email, VPN, admin consoles and remote access.
4. **Accounts:** individual accounts only; shared accounts forbidden. Admin rights separated from daily accounts.
5. **Least privilege:** access granted by role, reviewed every 6 months.
6. **Leavers:** accounts disabled the day of departure.
7. **Incidents:** suspected compromise is reported to the security contact immediately.
